Allow automatic Spotify capture?

This panel will capture Spotify output now and on future opens or reloads, without asking again. No microphone, saved audio, or uploads. macOS audio permission is still required.

Choose Disable visualizer in the panel controls to revoke this choice and stop capture. Closing the panel also stops capture; future opens can start again while permission remains enabled.
